Control Roku from your phone — no paid app

Roku speaks an open protocol (ECP) over the local network. Remote97 uses it so your phone navigates and types on any Roku — no subscription, no "premium" remote unlock.

4 min read · Android, Windows & iOS

Roku devices expose the External Control Protocol (ECP) on the local network. Plenty of apps charge for "pro" remote features; there's no need. Your phone already has everything required.

What you can do

  • Navigation — D-pad, select, back, home, replay, info
  • Playback — play, pause, rewind, fast-forward
  • On-screen keyboard — type channel searches and logins
  • Quick app launch

Steps

1

Install Remote97

From Google Play, App Store or Microsoft Store.

2

Same WiFi as the Roku

ECP is local-only; both devices on the same network.

3

Open Remote97 → discover

It finds Roku via SSDP. Tap to connect.

4

Navigate & type

Use the pad and the keyboard to control channels.

No account, no upsell. Remote97's Roku control is a standard local protocol — there is nothing to subscribe to and nothing to unlock.

Which Roku works?

Any Roku with network access — Express, Premiere, Ultra, Streaming Stick and Roku TVs. If it appears in Roku's own mobile app, it speaks ECP and works here.
