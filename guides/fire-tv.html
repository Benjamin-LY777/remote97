Control Amazon Fire TV from your phone over ADB

Amazon has no local remote API, but Fire TV runs Android underneath — and Android speaks ADB. Enable debugging once and your phone becomes a full keypad: navigation, playback, volume and app shortcuts.

5 min read · Android, Windows & iOS

A Fire TV Stick or Cube is a small Android box that plugs straight into an HDMI port. There is no cloud account handshake and no vendor SDK: the device listens for Android Debug Bridge connections on TCP port 5555 on your own network. That is the same channel Home Assistant uses for its official Android TV integration.

What you can do

  • Navigation — D-pad, select, back, home and menu
  • Playback — play, pause, rewind, fast-forward, previous / next
  • Volume — absolute level plus mute
  • App shortcuts — Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube, Disney+, Plex, Spotify
  • Power — screen off (sleep) and wake back to the home screen

Before you start: enable ADB debugging

1

Unlock Developer Options

On the Fire TV go to Settings → My Fire TV → About and click Fire TV seven times.

2

Turn on ADB debugging

Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → ADB debugging → On.

3

Note the IP address

Settings → My Fire TV → About → Network shows the address and the Wi-Fi network it is on.

4

Add it in Remote97

Open Remote97 → add device → Player → Amazon Fire TV, then enter the IP (port is fixed at 5555).

5

Allow the key on the TV

The first connection shows “Allow USB debugging?”. Choose Allow and tick “Always allow” — Remote97 stores its key, so you only do this once.

Only one ADB client at a time. If another tool (an IDE, a debug app, Home Assistant) holds the connection, Remote97 will report the device as busy. Close the other client and refresh.

No input switching — by design

A Fire TV is not a TV. It sits on one HDMI input and stays there, so “switch to HDMI 3” is a job for the television or the receiver. Remote97 deliberately does not show an input selector for Fire TV: a button that cannot do anything is worse than no button. Put the TV on the right input in a scene and let the Fire TV handle everything after that.

Known limits

  • Deep sleep drops the network. In full standby the Wi-Fi radio turns off and port 5555 closes; the app then shows the device offline rather than faking a connection.
  • Wi-Fi only. Fire TV Sticks have no Ethernet port (the Cube has one), so keep both devices on the same access point and avoid guest-network isolation.
  • No volume arbitration. The level Remote97 sets is the Fire TV’s own media stream. If your TV or soundbar controls volume over HDMI-CEC, the two can disagree — pick one place to change volume.
