Automation

Set up one-tap "Movie night" scenes

Stop juggling three remotes to watch a film. A scene runs a sequence across your devices in a single tap: receiver on, TV on, input switched, volume set.

5 min read · Android, Windows & iOS

A "scene" in Remote97 is a saved sequence of actions across multiple devices. One tap triggers all of them in order — exactly like the macros expensive control systems sell for hundreds of dollars.

A classic "Movie night" scene

  • AV receiver powers on and switches to Apple TV / HDMI input
  • TV powers on
  • Volume set to a sensible level
  • (Optional) Lights / other devices — anything Remote97 can reach

Steps

1

Add your devices

Discover and connect your AVR, TV and streamer (see the device guides).

2

Create a scene

In Remote97, open Scenes and start a new one. Give it a name like "Movie night".

3

Add ordered actions

Pick each device and the action: receiver → power on → HDMI 2; TV → power on; receiver → volume 35.

4

Save & run

One tap now powers and configures the whole setup. Build a "Good night" scene to power everything off just as easily.

Ideas beyond movies: "Music everywhere" groups your speakers (see whole-home audio); "Quiet late night" drops volume and switches to stereo. Any sequence you repeat is worth a scene.

Requirements

  • Devices added to Remote97 and reachable on the local network
  • That's it — scenes are a free feature, no hub required
