Multi-room

Whole-home audio: group Sonos, BluOS & WiiM

Speakers from different brands shouldn't live in different apps. Remote97 brings Sonos, Bluesound (BluOS) and WiiM into one screen so you can group and control them together.

5 min read · Android, Windows & iOS

You bought speakers over the years — a Sonos in the kitchen, a Bluesound in the den, a WiiM in the bedroom. Each has its own app. Remote97 unifies them on the local network.

What you can do

  • See every speaker in one place — no app-hopping
  • Group zones — play the same thing everywhere, or split into zones
  • Volume & tracks — control playback for each group
  • No cloud dependency — commands stay on your network

Steps

1

Install Remote97

From Google Play, App Store or Microsoft Store.

2

All speakers on the same WiFi

They must share the network with your phone.

3

Open Remote97 → discover

Sonos (UPnP/SSDP), BluOS and WiiM are detected automatically.

4

Group & play

Select speakers into a zone and control volume/tracks together.

One screen, many brands. Instead of three manufacturer apps, you get a single consistent interface for every speaker you own.

Supported platforms

Sonos (UPnP/SOAP), Bluesound (BluOS) and WiiM / Arylic (LinkPlay) are detected and controllable on the local network.
