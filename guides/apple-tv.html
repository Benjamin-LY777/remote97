Best Apple TV remote alternative for iPhone

The Siri Remote is tiny and easy to lose. Remote97 turns your iPhone into a dependable Apple TV remote — with a real keyboard for typing passwords and searching.

5 min read · iPhone, Android & Windows

Apple TV uses the Media Remote Protocol (MRP) over the local network. Remote97 speaks it, so your phone becomes a full Apple TV remote — navigation, playback, and an on-screen keyboard.

What you can do

  • D-pad, select, menu, home, play/pause
  • On-screen keyboard — type Apple ID passwords and search queries from your phone
  • Volume — if your TV is reachable on the same network
  • App switching — jump straight to apps

Steps

1

Install Remote97 on your iPhone

From the App Store (also Android & Windows).

2

Same WiFi as the Apple TV

Local network control — no internet required.

3

Open Remote97 → discover

It finds the Apple TV via mDNS. Tap to connect (a one-time pairing prompt may appear on screen).

4

Type with the keyboard

Tap the keyboard button to enter credentials and searches with your phone.

Note: On iOS, local-network discovery needs the "Local Network" permission — Remote97 prompts for it on first use. If discovery finds nothing, check that permission in iOS Settings.

Why a phone remote?

A phone keyboard is dramatically faster than the on-screen one, and you're far less likely to lose your phone than the Siri Remote. Keep both — they work side by side.
