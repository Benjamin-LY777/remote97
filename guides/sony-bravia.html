Turn your Android into a Sony BRAVIA remote

The physical BRAVIA remote always disappears at the worst moment. Your Android phone can do everything it does — over the local network.

5 min read · Android, Windows & iOS

Sony BRAVIA TVs have built-in network control. Remote97 uses it so your phone becomes a full remote: power, input, volume, and — most usefully — an on-screen keyboard for typing passwords and searching.

What you can do

  • Power on / off / standby
  • Input / source switching — HDMI 1–4, YouTube, Netflix and other apps
  • Volume & mute
  • On-screen text input — type accounts and searches with your phone keyboard
  • D-pad & navigation — move, select, back, home

Steps

1

Install Remote97

From Google Play, App Store or Microsoft Store. No account needed.

2

Connect to the same WiFi

Your phone and the BRAVIA must be on the same network.

3

Open Remote97 and discover

It finds the TV via SSDP/mDNS. Tap it to connect.

4

Control & type

Use the on-screen pad to navigate, and the keyboard button to type.

Tip: The keyboard feature alone is worth it. Logging into a new streaming app on a TV with a 4-way pad is painful — Remote97 lets you type normally from your phone.

Which TVs work?

Android-based BRAVIA models with network control (most 2015+ BRAVIA Android TVs and Google TVs). If the TV appears in Sony's own Video & TV SideView / BRAVIA app, it will work here too.
