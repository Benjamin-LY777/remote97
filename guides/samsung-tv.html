TV

Control Samsung TV without the physical remote

The Samsung remote is lost, in the washing machine, or out of batteries. Your phone on the same WiFi can take over completely.

5 min read · Android, Windows & iOS

Samsung TVs respond to commands over the local network. Remote97 turns your phone into that remote — no replacement handset, no smart-hub box, no account.

What you can do

  • Power on / off — even from standby, over the network
  • Input / source — HDMI, TV, apps
  • Volume & mute
  • Navigation & text input — D-pad, back, home, and a phone keyboard for typing

Steps

1

Install Remote97

Grab it from Google Play, App Store or Microsoft Store.

2

Connect to the same WiFi

Phone and TV on the same network (not a VPN).

3

Open Remote97 → discover

It finds the Samsung TV via SSDP/mDNS. Tap to connect.

4

Control

Power, input, volume and keyboard all from your phone.

Why not just buy a replacement remote? A phone remote is free, always charged (probably), and doubles as a keyboard — which a $15 replacement remote can't do.

Which TVs work?

Most Samsung Smart TVs with network control (Tizen-based models from recent years). If your TV shows up in Samsung's own SmartThings / Smart View app, it's controllable here too.
