Remote97 turns the phone or PC you already own into a universal remote for your home cinema. It talks to your equipment directly over your local Wi-Fi network — no control hub, no professional installation, no monthly fees.

## Highlights

- **No hub, no extra hardware** — runs as an app on devices you already have and connects straight to your equipment over LAN.
- **Automatic discovery** — finds supported devices on your network by itself. No typing IP addresses.
- **One unified remote** — volume, input source, power, sound modes, EQ and on-screen text input, all in one place.
- **Multi-room audio** — group Sonos, Bluesound (BluOS), WiiM and Arylic speakers and play music in sync across rooms.
- **Made yours** — arrange devices into homes and rooms, choose from multiple UI themes including glass and ink-wash styles.
- **Windows with global hotkeys** — control everything from the keyboard without leaving your seat.
- **Privacy first** — no cloud, no account, no telemetry. Everything stays on your local network.
- **Free.**
